# 🌸 Partner Menstrual Cycle & Care Tracker

A loving, empathetic, and scientifically grounded companion web application and mobile Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to track your girlfriend's menstrual cycle, calculate upcoming periods, and provide caring guidance across all 4 phases.

---

### ✨ Features:

1. **🩸 The 4 Cycle Phases & Partner Care Guides**:
   - **Menstrual Phase** (Days 1–5): Comfort foods, cramps support, heating pads, warm tea, emotional gentleness.
   - **Follicular Phase** (Days 6–13): Energy surge, creative projects, adventurous date nights, upbeat stamina.
   - **Ovulation Phase** (Days 14–16): Peak confidence, fertile window, radiance, romantic evenings.
   - **Luteal Phase / PMS** (Days 17–28): Extra patience, PMS relief, comfort carbs, massages, reassurance.

2. **⏳ Accurate Next Period & Ovulation Calculator**:
   - Live countdown (*"in X days"*, *"Tomorrow"*, or *"Today!"*).
   - Fertility window and ovulation peak tracking.
   - Interactive 28-day cycle dial and progress breakdown.

3. **🔔 Phase Reminders & Alarms**:
   - Browser push notifications on entering each phase and 2 days before her period.
   - 1-Click test alerts for each phase.
   - Export to Phone Calendar (`.ics`) for Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, and Outlook with pre-set alarm notifications.

4. **📱 Installable Progressive Web App (PWA)**:
   - Installable on iPhone (Safari) and Android (Chrome) as a native app with offline support.

5. **💬 1-Click Ready-to-Send Care Texts**:
   - Thoughtful, caring messages personalized to her current phase ready to copy and send.
