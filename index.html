The 4 Cycle Phases & Partner Care Guide

Know her hormonal shifts, how she feels, and exact steps you can take to make her feel cherished.

Phase 1 Days 1 - 5

🩸 Menstrual Phase

Her Body: Estrogen & progesterone drop. Uterus contracts to shed lining. Cramps, lower energy, sensitivity, headache.

❤️ How to Care for Her

  • Prepare a heating pad or hot water bag
  • Bring dark chocolate & warm herbal tea
  • Plan cozy movie nights at home
  • Offer gentle lower back or foot rubs
  • Avoid arguing; listen with affection
Status Upcoming
Phase 2 Days 6 - 13

🌱 Follicular Phase

Her Body: FSH spurs egg maturation. Estrogen rises steadily. Energy, mood, mental clarity & optimism are surging!

✨ How to Care for Her

  • Take her out on adventurous date nights
  • Support new hobbies or creative ideas
  • Go for workouts, walks, or sports together
  • She feels social; invite friends over
  • Her stamina and good mood are at peak
Status Upcoming
Phase 3 Days 14 - 16

🌟 Ovulation Phase

Her Body: LH surge releases egg. Highest estrogen, peak testosterone, radiant skin, highest libido & fertility.

🔥 How to Care for Her

  • Shower her with sincere compliments
  • Plan romantic dinners or spontaneous trips
  • High intimate connection & vitality
  • Mind fertility window depending on family goals
  • Celebrate her radiant energy
Status Upcoming
Phase 4 Days 17 - 28

🍂 Luteal Phase (PMS)

Her Body: Progesterone peaks then plunges. Bloating, mood swings, irritability, food cravings, insomnia, tender breasts.

🛡️ How to Care for Her

  • Extra patience: don't take snappy words personally
  • Help out proactively with chores and food
  • Stock magnesium-rich snacks & comfort carbs
  • Reassure her often; provide emotional safety
  • Keep stress and noisy demands to a minimum
